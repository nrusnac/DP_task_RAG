# 07 · Decision log

Each entry: the decision, the alternatives considered, and why. Numbers come from the prep smoke test on a Colab T4
(`prep/colab_smoke_test.ipynb`) or from this repo's eval.

| 18 | **No web search in CRAG; abstain** | web fallback (paper) | Matter-specific, confidential facts. "Not found" beats a fabricated figure |

| 23 | **Tuned the agent after a dry run** (router: "when in doubt, simple"; 1 sufficiency iteration; own reasoning labelled "Analysis:") | keep the first version | The first dry run sent targeted questions down the complex path (20–29 LLM calls, memo-length answers to narrow questions), and the faithfulness judge flagged unlabelled legal inferences |

| 25 | **Clearly relevant hits (≥ τ_high) bypass strip refinement and are passed whole** (superseded by #38) | refine everything to sentences | In the first Colab run, 3 of the 4 agent failures had the gold chunk retrieved and graded correct, then pruned to 73–475 words (enumerations of redline changes, clause text). | 26 | **Unanswerable-question eval (cross-matter negatives)** | answerable questions only | An answerable-only QA set can't reward abstention, only penalise it. ## Branch `agent-improvements`
Motivated by the final `main` run: the agent passed most often (0.967) but was the least faithful (0.50 vs 0.633 / 0.70) and answered more unanswerable questions (2/12 vs 1/12), and every number came from 30 questions that had also guided the design.

| 31 | **Rerank against the original sub-question after a rewrite** | rerank against the rewritten keywords (main) | τ is calibrated on question-vs-chunk scores; keyword-vs-chunk scores sit on a different scale, so rewritten rounds were graded with thresholds that didn't apply to them |
